Two problems, one habit: watch the data, then act on it.

Sign Language Detection System

2026 · Python · MediaPipe · SVM

A real-time system that reads hand gestures through a webcam and classifies them into sign language, built to narrow the communication gap for people who are deaf or hard of hearing.

  • Extracts 21 hand landmarks per frame with MediaPipe and converts them into feature vectors.
  • Trains a Support Vector Machine to classify gestures into predefined sign classes.
  • Runs low-latency prediction live, overlaying detected signs and landmarks on screen.
PythonMediaPipeSVMComputer vision

KrishiMitra — AI-Powered Farmer Assistance Platform

2025 · JavaScript · AI APIs · Smart India Hackathon

A multilingual crop advisory platform that gives farmers personalised recommendations, weather alerts, pest detection, and market-based ROI insight, built for the Smart India Hackathon 2025.

  • Combines advisory logic with real-time weather alerts and pest detection.
  • Designed offline-first and voice-enabled, so it stays usable with patchy connectivity or low literacy.
  • Selected in the Institute-Level Round of Smart India Hackathon 2025.
